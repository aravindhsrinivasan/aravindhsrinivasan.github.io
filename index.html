How I approach AI products

Trust is a feature

The questions I want answered before calling a demo a product.

Start with the user, not the model

Understand the task, the current workaround and the cost of being wrong. Use examples of failure to decide where AI can help and where it should not act.

Set the bar before the result

Write down success criteria, non-goals and unacceptable failures. A launch decision should not depend on moving the goalposts after seeing an evaluation.

Use the right kind of evidence

Check deterministic requirements with code, use expert review for judgment, and calibrate model-based evaluation rather than treating it as ground truth.

Make the approval boundary visible

Distinguish reading, drafting and acting. Show uncertainty, explain tool failures and require approval before a consequential action.

Know what would make you stop

Plan feedback, regression checks and rollback conditions. Report inconclusive results when the evidence does not support a decision.

The product judgment: know when not to ship. Quality criteria, human review and a rollback plan matter as much as the happy-path demo.
